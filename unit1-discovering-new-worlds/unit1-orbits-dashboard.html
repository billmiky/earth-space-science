🌠 Phenomenon: Why does orbital velocity increase at perihelion, and why does Earth’s orbit appear circular despite being an ellipse?

1 · Pattern observed — How does the separation between the two foci change the orbit’s shape?

2 · Evidence from simulation — How does the \(e\) value relate to the change in orbital velocity between perihelion and aphelion?

3 · Mechanism — Use \(F_g = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}\) and kinetic vs. potential energy to explain the speed change.
