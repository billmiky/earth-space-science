7 Recurring Regents Moves

High-leverage question structures that appear on every exam.

  1. Pick the row. Match a claim to supporting evidence in a 2–3 column table.
  2. Direction of change. Read a graph and state how one variable changes with another, quoting numbers.
  3. Compare two scenarios. Before vs. after, two locations, or two engineering solutions.
  4. Put a model in order. Sequence steps (subduction, carbon pathway, solar core to surface).
  5. Judge a solution. Select which option best meets criteria and name one trade-off.
  6. Systems chain. Trace how a change in one sphere ripples into another (O₂ & BIF, ice & albedo, CO₂ & temp).
  7. Extend a cycle. Predict the next tide, eclipse, or orbital position from a repeating pattern.
