How to use this
  1. Filter by unit, 5E topic, standard, skill, or type. View shows the question full-size with its key.
  2. Tick Add on the questions you want. They collect in the bar at the bottom, in the order you pick them; use ↑ ↓ to reorder.
  3. Preview shows the quiz in order. Print makes a numbered test (add the answer key at the end if you like); choose “Save as PDF” to get a PDF.
  4. Copy IDs copies the question list. Paste it into the ESS Tracker under Assessments → New assessment to grade it there, with the keys and ZipGrade numbers filled in.

🔒 = also on a New Visions interim assessment. New Visions asks that those questions not be used for class practice, so interim results show what students really know.

    Material marked “from earlier in this set” is included so each question makes sense. Use Hide for this printout on anything a question doesn't need.
